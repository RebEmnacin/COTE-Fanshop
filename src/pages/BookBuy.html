CLASSROOM OF THE ELITE

Year 3 Volume 3

It’s time for this year’s Uninhabited Island Special Exam. Classes will face off in a four-day game of survival, and the first class to fall will incur a penalty in the expulsion selection, so every move counts! Students will move through the battleground, collecting food and bullets while duking it out with students from the other classes. And with GPS making their every move visible to the commanding officers in each class, strategy and group coordination is key for survival! In an exam where one wrong move can doom them to failure, can Ayanokouji lead his class to victory?.
